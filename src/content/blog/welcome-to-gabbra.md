I see photography as an interruption of time: a fleeting expression, a passing shadow, a coincidence or an otherwise ordinary moment worth keeping.

GabbRa brings together fragments of life captured through my perspective, in photography, in flight with FPV drones, and in film.

This blog will hold the stories behind the work. **Welcome to GabbRa.**
