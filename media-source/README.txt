GABBRA  -  CONTENT FOLDER
=========================

Drop your files in here, then tell Claude: "content is ready".
Claude resizes, optimises and places everything on the website.
Nothing in here goes online as it is; the big originals stay on your computer.


PHOTOGRAPHS   ->   photo/
--------------------------
Make ONE FOLDER PER PROJECT OR SUBJECT and put its photographs inside.

   photo/
      lisbon-nights/        <- the folder name becomes the project title
         IMG_0001.jpg
         IMG_0002.jpg
      coastline/
         ...

 - JPG, PNG, TIFF or WebP, full size is fine (bigger is better).
 - Photos show in file-name order. Name them 01-..., 02-... to control the order.
 - The FIRST photo in a folder is the cover (you can change this later).
 - Optional: add a small file called project.json to a folder to set a
   proper title, subject, year, description and print details.
   There is an example in photo/_example-project/ . Folders starting with
   an underscore (_) are ignored.


THE COLLABORATION CLIP   ->   film/collaborations/
--------------------------------------------------
Put the 1-minute video here (MP4, H.264 is best; keep it under about 40 MB).
Optionally add a poster image (poster.jpg) and a project.json for the
title, year and credits. Example inside the folder.


ABOUT YOU   ->   about/
-----------------------
A portrait (portrait.jpg) and a few paragraphs of text (story.txt) if you
want them on the About and Behind the Lens pages.


THINGS TO TELL CLAUDE AFTER YOU DROP FILES IN
---------------------------------------------
 - Which photos (if any) you would like to offer as PRINTS, and sizes, paper,
   edition size and price if you have them.
 - Your Instagram name.
 - Who is credited on the collaboration clip (and in what role).
